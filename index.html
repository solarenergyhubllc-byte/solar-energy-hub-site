Columbia, Missouri | Phase A in planning

Wind, shade and switchgrass on a Missouri research farm.

Solar Energy Hub, LLC is planning a wind and agrivoltaic research campus on University of Missouri South Farms land: ten short wind turbines, a solar shade barn for cattle, battery backup and switchgrass fuels research.

What we plan to build

Original concept drawing, not to scale. It shows the idea only. Nothing is designed, surveyed or filed.

  1. 1

    Ten wind turbines

    Ten turbines of 500 kW each, every one under 190 ft to the blade tip (five are drawn here). At central-Missouri wind speeds that is about 26 to 40 MWh a day. We will measure the wind on site for 12 months before ordering anything.

  2. 2

    Solar shade barn

    An open-sided barn over 2 acres: a solar roof on weatherproof steel posts and concrete bases, so cattle stay in the shade and researchers can study heat stress. About 0.6 MW of panels and 2 to 3 MWh a day.

  3. 3

    Battery storage

    Storage for off-grid backup of critical loads. It is sized after a load study.

  4. 4

    Grid tie-in

    A proposed connection to the existing overhead lines. We understand they belong to Boone Electric Cooperative. No application has been filed.

  5. 5

    Cattle and research

    Black cattle stand in the shade, so researchers can study heat stress. Switchgrass for biogas and ethanol research, plus farm robots and AI implements, share the same acres.

The land

University of Missouri CAFNR South Farms, 250 to 300 acres, offered verbally and not yet in writing. Phase A's ten turbines need about 140 to 200 acres of spacing, shared with farming and research.

Wind siting

Each turbine is kept under 190 feet to the blade tip, and the ten need about 140 to 200 acres of spacing, away from Highway 63, homes and roads. A siting study and an airport-distance check come first.

Backup power for a critical load Proposal only

KOMU-TV8 is a short distance away. Storage could keep a critical load running off-grid. There is no agreement and no load study.

Three phases

Phase A | 2026 to 2030

Pilot campus

Ten 500 kW wind turbines, a 2-acre shade barn, battery storage and switchgrass research. $28M target from grants, partner equipment and equity. No debt.

Phase B | 2031 and after

Build-out to 36 MW

About 36 MW of wind and solar, for example 2 towers plus 24 MW of solar (illustrative). Built by partner-funded entities at an estimated $53M to $85M.

Phase C | Vision

A 1.0 to 1.5 GW hub

A hub that could use a turbine. Fuel and partners are undefined. It is not costed and not part of the current raise.

What the $28M buys and who can fund it

Phase A needs $28M and uses no debt. It comes from three kinds of funders, and the money arrives in stages as each gate is passed. Nothing has been raised yet, and every figure here is a target, not a commitment.
