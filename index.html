Solar trees, shade and switchgrass on a Missouri research farm.

Solar Energy Hub, LLC is planning a solar and agrivoltaic research campus on a university research farm near an existing broadcast tower: solar trees that could help power the tower, shade for cattle and other animals, battery backup and switchgrass fuels research.

What we plan to build

Original concept drawing, not to scale. It shows the idea only. Nothing is designed, surveyed or filed.

  1. 1

    Solar trees

    Solar trees of 40 to 50 kW each, standing in pasture. Each one makes power and casts shade. How many we build depends on the tower's load, which we have not measured, and on the research design.

  2. 2

    Shade and research

    Cattle and other animals stand in the shade, so researchers can study heat stress. Switchgrass for biogas and ethanol research share the same acres.

  3. 3

    Battery storage

    Storage for off-grid backup of critical loads. It is sized after a load study.

  4. 4

    Power link to the tower

    A proposed private line to help offset the power the tower uses. There is no agreement with the tower's owner, no load study and nothing filed.

  5. 5

    Grid tie-in

    A proposed connection to the existing overhead lines. We understand they belong to the local electric utility. No application has been filed.

The solar tree idea

The solar tree is the building block: a tall structure over pasture that makes power and shades animals on the same acre. Everything here is a concept to be tested with a university research partner and engineers. Nothing is designed or built, and further technical details will be shared with partners under a confidentiality agreement.

Power

Solar power from each tree, sized later with quotes and a load study.

Shade and research

Shade for cattle and other animals so researchers can measure heat stress, while switchgrass and other crops grow nearby.

Measured results

A first research phase to measure animal comfort, grass growth, power output and cost, and to publish what we learn.

Crop and materials research on the same land

Side-by-side plots of switchgrass and other crops, each with and without tree shade, designed with university researchers. Yields for Missouri are not yet proven.

  5. 5

The land

A university research farm, 250 to 300 acres, discussed verbally and not yet in writing. The solar trees are meant to stand over working pasture, so farming and research continue beneath them.

Power for the tower Proposal only

We would like to help offset the power the tower uses. We have not measured its load and have no agreement with its owner. Storage could keep a critical load running off-grid.

Wind, later

Wind is not in Phase A. It may return as a later option once on-site data exist. Measured tall-tower wind data for Columbia exist: Forecasting Wind Speeds at Tall Tower Heights Within Missouri, University of Missouri doctoral dissertation, 2022. Those instruments sit at 68 m and higher, above short-turbine heights.

Three phases

Phase A | in planning

Pilot campus

Solar trees, shade for animals, battery storage, a proposed power link to the tower and switchgrass research. Budget is being re-costed. No debt.

Phase B | later

Build-out

More solar trees and research partners if Phase A works. Not planned in detail and not costed.

Phase C | Vision

A larger research hub

A bigger energy and research hub. Partners and fuel are undefined. It is not costed and not part of the current raise.

How Phase A can be funded

Phase A uses no debt. We are re-costing the budget after a change of plan, and we will publish a figure once quotes arrive. Nothing has been raised yet, and every figure on this page is a plan, not a commitment.

Where the money could come from

  • Grants. Federal, state, university and foundation programs for agrivoltaics, solar and bioenergy research. None awarded yet.
  • Partner in-kind. Equipment and services from solar, storage and technology partners. Terms are unsigned.
  • Equity. Investors and strategic partners.

Later stages wait on a gate: a signed site agreement, a measured tower load, an interconnection study and the first grant awards.

What the money would buy

  • Solar trees, foundations and installation
  • Battery storage and backup, to be sized
  • A power link to the tower and a grid tie-in, if agreed
  • Switchgrass and fuels research plots
  • Measurement, permits, legal and an operating reserve

Dollar amounts will be added once quotes and the tower's load study are in.
